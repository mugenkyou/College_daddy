📌 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is College Daddy, and how does it help students?

College Daddy is your ultimate college companion, designed to help students track grades, organize assignments, and stay on top of their academic journey.

2. Can I track my GPA and grades with College Daddy?

Yes! College Daddy provides a simple GPA calculator and grade tracker to help you monitor your academic progress.

3. Is College Daddy free to use?

Yes, College Daddy is completely free for students. No hidden fees or subscriptions!

4. How can I organize my assignments and deadlines?

College Daddy offers a built-in planner where you can set reminders, track assignments, and stay ahead of deadlines.

5. Does College Daddy work for all colleges?

Absolutely! Whether you're in a university, community college, or online program, College Daddy is designed to help students in any academic setting.

6. Is College Daddy available on mobile?

Currently, College Daddy is a web-based platform, but a mobile app version is in development. Stay tuned!

7. Does College Daddy have all notes for my subjects?

Not yet! We're working on adding a comprehensive notes section, but since every course is different, it's a bit of a challenge. Stay tuned for updates!

8. Are College Daddy's roadmaps reliable?

Yes! Our roadmaps are designed by industry professionals and academic experts, ensuring that students follow the best learning paths possible.

9. Can I share my progress with friends or professors?

Currently, progress tracking is personal, but we're exploring ways to add collaborative features in the future.

10. How can I suggest new features for College Daddy?

We love feedback! You can send your ideas through our contact page or social media channels. Your input helps shape the future of College Daddy!
