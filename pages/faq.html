Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about College Daddy tools and resources.

Is my data safe?

Yes! College Daddy is a client-side platform. All your academic data, including grades and timer history, is stored locally in your browser. We don't have servers that store your private information.

How do I update my saved data?

Most tools like the Attendance tracker and CGPA Tracker have "Save" buttons. When you click them, the data is committed to your browser's local storage and will be available the next time you visit.

Can I use it on mobile?

Absolutely! College Daddy is fully responsive and optimized for mobile browsers. You can even "Add to Home Screen" on your mobile device to use it like a native app.

Is this project open source?

College Daddy is a community-driven project. We welcome contributions from other student developers! Check out our GitHub repository if you're interested in adding new features.

What new features are coming?

We are constantly working on new tools. Upcoming features include a collaborative notes sharing portal, semester-wise course checklists, and integrated placement preparation resources.
