Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about College Daddy tools and resources.

What is College Daddy?

College Daddy is your ultimate college companion, designed to help students track grades, organize assignments, and stay on top of their academic journey.

Can I track my GPA?

Yes! College Daddy provides a simple GPA calculator and grade tracker to help you monitor your academic progress seamlessly.

Is College Daddy free?

Yes, College Daddy is completely free for students. No hidden fees or subscriptions!

How can I organize assignments?

College Daddy offers a built-in planner where you can set reminders, track assignments, and stay ahead of deadlines.

Does it work for all colleges?

Absolutely! Whether you're in a university, community college, or online program, College Daddy is designed for any academic setting.

Is it available on mobile?

Currently, College Daddy is a web-based platform, but a mobile app version is in development. Stay tuned!

Do you have all notes?

Not yet! We're working on adding a comprehensive notes section, but since every course is different, it's an ongoing challenge. Stay tuned for updates!

Are roadmaps reliable?

Yes! Our roadmaps are designed by industry professionals and academic experts, ensuring you follow the best learning paths possible.

Can I share my progress?

Currently, progress tracking is personal, but we're exploring ways to add collaborative features in the future.

How can I suggest features?

We love feedback! You can send your ideas through our contact page or social media channels. Your input helps shape the future of College Daddy!

Is my data safe?

Yes! College Daddy is a client-side platform. All your academic data is stored locally in your browser. We don't have servers that store your private information.
