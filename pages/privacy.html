Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

At College Daddy, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we handle your data and ensure your information remains secure while you use our tools.

Data Collection

College Daddy is designed to be privacy-first. Most of our tools, including the CGPA Calculator and Study Timer, operate entirely within your browser.

  • Local Storage: Your grades, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device using browser local storage.
  • No Backend Storage: We do not store your academic records on our servers.

Analytics

We use minimal analytics to understand how our tools are used and to improve the student experience.

  • Anonymized Data: All usage data is anonymized and contains no personally identifiable information.
  • Usage Patterns: We track which tools are most popular to prioritize future updates.

Security

Since your data lives on your device, its security depends on your browser's security. We recommend using modern, updated browsers to ensure maximum protection.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, feel free to reach out via our feedback form available in the footer.
